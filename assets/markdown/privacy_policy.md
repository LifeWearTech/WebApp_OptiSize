## 1. Introduction
Life Wear Technologies respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the OptiSize App handles user information.

## 2. Information We Collect
The App does not collect, store, or transmit personal data.

We do not collect:
- Names
- Emails
- Phone numbers
- Location data
- Photos or videos
- Device identifiers
- Analytics
- Payment information

## 3. Camera Use
Camera use is limited to barcode scanning and AR measurement. No images are captured or stored.

## 4. Local Data Storage
All product data is stored locally in a JSON file.

## 5. Third‑Party Services
The App does not use Firebase, Google Analytics, AdMob, cloud storage, or advertising networks.

## 6. Children’s Privacy
The App is intended for users 14+.

## 7. Security
No personal data is collected; therefore, no personal data is at risk.
